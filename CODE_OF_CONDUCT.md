# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in RescueLink a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience;
- focusing on what is best for the community and RescueLink's mission.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery and sexual attention or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- sharing credentials, access tokens, exact private addresses, phone numbers, or other sensitive data;
- spam, deceptive contributions, intentionally misleading reports, or attempts to manipulate project workflows;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that are inconsistent with this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to temporarily or permanently limit participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, harmful, or unsafe.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all RescueLink community spaces and whenever an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

Examples include GitHub issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project-related social media conversations, and public or private communication conducted on behalf of RescueLink.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the repository owner using the contact information available on the [owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/ismailbarankarasu).

Do not include private personal information in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible. The privacy and safety of the reporter will be respected to the extent reasonably possible.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, severity, repetition, impact, and whether corrective action is possible.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will follow these Community Impact Guidelines when determining consequences.

### 1. Correction

**Community impact:** Inappropriate language or behavior that is unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence:** A private or public warning explaining the violation and expected correction. A public apology may be requested when appropriate.

### 2. Warning

**Community impact:** A violation through a single incident or a series of actions.

**Consequence:** A warning with consequences for continued behavior. Interaction with the people involved may be restricted for a specified period.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community impact:** A serious violation or sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** A temporary ban from interaction or public communication with the project for a specified period.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community impact:** Repeated violations, harassment, aggression, or behavior that threatens community safety.

**Consequence:** Permanent removal from project participation and community interaction.
